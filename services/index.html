Federal & restricted networks

Built for networks the cloud can't reach.

Roundhouse deploys inside your authorization boundary. Nothing about it needs to leave.

Hosted MCP platforms assume connectivity you don't have and data flows you can't approve. Roundhouse was designed the other way around: everything runs on your hardware, and the source is open for your security team to read.

  • HostingFully self-hosted. No SaaS control plane, no license server, no phone-home.
  • OfflineWorks air-gapped — internal registry mirrors, bundled docs, custom CA bundles for TLS-inspecting proxies.
  • ImagesSupports hardened, non-root, distroless runtime bases for generated servers.
  • BuildsRootless image builds on Kubernetes, RBAC-scoped, with no Docker socket exposed.
  • AccessOIDC single sign-on, role mappings, per-tool scopes, and tokens encrypted at rest.
  • AuditEvery state-changing action is recorded with the acting user, target, and detail.
  • SourceApache-2.0 — inspect it, scan it, fork it.

Straight talk: Roundhouse is software you run, not a cloud service, so it inherits your environment's authorization rather than carrying its own. We won't claim a certification we don't hold — we will give your ISSO the architecture, data-flow, and configuration detail they need to assess it.

Questions

Asked at the
ticket window

Is Roundhouse still free and open source?

Yes. The platform is Apache-2.0 and every tier runs the same software. Paid tiers buy you people and commitments, not features held hostage.

Do you need access to our network?

No. Most support happens over tickets and screen shares, and for air-gapped environments we work from sanitized logs and configuration you choose to send. On-site work is available by arrangement.

How is pricing structured?

Production support is an annual subscription per deployment. Enterprise / Federal and professional services are quoted against your scope. Tell us what you're running and we'll come back with a number.

Can we buy through an existing contract or reseller?

Usually, yes. Tell us which vehicle, reseller, or prime contractor you prefer to work through and we'll sort out the path with your contracting office.

We only need help once — do we need a subscription?

No. Professional services are available standalone: a deployment, an air-gap enablement, or a batch of custom MCP servers can each be its own fixed-scope engagement.

Who actually answers the ticket?

An engineer from Karmatek, the team that designs and builds Roundhouse. There is no tier-one script between you and someone who can read the code.
